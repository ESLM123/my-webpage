How AI is Changing Language, Identity and You

Inspired by Afrakoma Hmensa's Research

AI Is Everywhere

From our phones, laptops to search engines, AI is already shaping how we live. We use it daily even when we don’t notice it.

Whose Language Does AI Speak?

Most AI tools like Google Translate or DeepL work better in dominant languages like English or Spanish. Why? Because that’s where most of the training data comes from. Hundreds of African languages are often invisible in AI systems.

“If AI can’t recognize the language, can it truly study how people communicate?”

The Global AI Divide

Most AI is trained on data from the Global North, places like the U.S. and Europe. That leaves many countries, especially African countries, in the Global South underrepresented in how AI sees the world.

This creates digital inequality: the AI we use doesn’t reflect everyone’s voice or experience equally.

Accent-Neutralizing AI

Tools like Sanas and TomatoAi change people’s accents in real time. But:

  • Are we improving communication or erasing identity?
  • What happens when your real voice is filtered through AI?
“If AI changes the way you sound to fit in, does it also change your identity?”

Think Critically, Speak Authentically

In the digital age, we have the power to shape how AI understands us. Just like Aristotle’s ethos, pathos, and logos helped people communicate persuasively, we can use AI in ways that reflect our full selves.

AI shouldn’t strip us of our identities but should reflect our true selves, whether its our cultural background, voice, or language. In our digital age, we must decide how we allow AI to interact with us.
